## CV structure and evidence

Keep selectable text, ordinary paragraphs, a single column and clear headings.
Greenhouse lists images, complex tables, columns and contact information in
document headers or text boxes as possible extraction problems. Its resume
parser has a separate 2.5 MB limit. A visual heading in the document body is
compatible with this layout decision.
[Greenhouse parsing documentation](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse).

PDF and DOCX are supported by Greenhouse. Neither source proves that DOCX
universally performs better than a properly exported PDF.
[Supported formats](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-formats-for-resumes-cover-letters-and-other-candidate-uploads).

The user explicitly permits three pages. Keep this length and prioritize the
strongest recent production work on page one; do not compress the headline or
body into unreadable type. Stanford recommends readable 10–12 pt text; the
exported pages must be inspected rather than judged from source alone.
[Stanford resume guidance](https://careered.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj22801/files/media/file/developing_your_resume_handout.pdf).

Write concise accomplishment bullets: context, personal contribution and
supported result. STAR helps collect evidence; PAR/XYZ can express it more
compactly. Preserve qualifiers and collective attribution. Use qualitative
outcomes when no verified measurement exists.
[MIT accomplishment guidance](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes-writing-about-your-skills/),
[Google's XYZ guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/google_how-to-apply-for-a-job-at-google-activity-7297670002780868610-FI-Z).

Place central keywords beside relevant work instead of relying only on a skills
list. Adapt their emphasis to each target job while retaining truthful claims.
[Harvard professional resume guidance](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/hes-create-impactful-resumes-and-cover-letters/).

Report text extraction, visual review and source/PDF parity. Do not invent a
universal ATS score. Ashby's optional AI review uses employer-defined criteria;
that does not reproduce every employer's configuration.
[Ashby application review](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/ai-assisted-application-review).
