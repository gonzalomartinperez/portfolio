# A clear recruiter journey

Reviewed 2026-09-19. This is an evidence-informed editorial and navigation
decision, not a promise of interviews or a universal recruiter preference.

## Sources and limits

- [GitHub: using a profile to enhance a resume](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/tutorials/using-your-github-profile-to-enhance-your-resume)
  recommends a concise introduction, selected relevant projects and readable
  repositories with setup, examples and testing information. This applies directly
  to the engineering evidence linked from this site, rather than making the
  portfolio itself a substitute for evidence.
- [NN/g: creating a portfolio](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-design-portfolios/)
  emphasizes scannability, contribution, constraints and outcomes. Its research
  concerns UX hiring, not AI engineering; transferring that presentation pattern
  here is an editorial inference, not a measured hiring conversion result.
- [NN/g: recruiter insights](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-hiring-insights/)
  adds a recruiter perspective on demonstrating relevant work. It is a qualitative
  design-industry source, not proof that a particular wording improves ATS ranking.
- [web.dev: website navigation](https://web.dev/articles/website-navigation)
  supports semantic navigation, meaningful landmarks and robust keyboard use.
  The animation must not become the only route to professional information.
- [W3C: animation from interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)
  explains why nonessential interaction-triggered motion needs an off alternative.
  The existing reduced-motion and static catalogue paths remain first-class.

These sources were checked now; their original publication dates vary. They do
not establish a special 2026 formula or support claims about precise recruiter
attention spans, ATS keyword density or guaranteed employment outcomes.

## Decisions for this portfolio

1. **Introduce a person, not a keyword wall.** Lead with Gonzalo's name, AI Software
   Engineer positioning and what he builds. Use friendly first-person language
   in both locales. Keep informal warmth, not jokes, superlatives or corporate jargon.
2. **Separate summary from detail.** Home introduces the work; Work explains scope,
   responsibility and qualified outcomes; Filomena supplies a deeper case study.
   About covers background and working style instead of repeating every bullet.
3. **Show engineering principles honestly.** The hero footer describes scalability,
   maintainability and software quality. They are principles, not measurements.
   Production figures stay beside their project context and attribution.
4. **Make the toolkit navigable.** Keep applied AI first in category priority;
   sort names alphabetically within categories. Use genuine local marks and
   illustrative icons for concepts. Do not turn pending knowledge into experience.
   Preserve search, visible labels and direct links to professional context.
5. **Keep the visual identity useful.** Preserve the interactive scene, but reserve
   header clearance and enough document space for the final grid. Do not shrink
   labels to fit an arbitrary viewport or introduce a nested scroll trap.
6. **Present evidence as documents.** Academic cards separate title, provenance,
   file metadata and open/download actions. Grade chips retain exact values and
   distinguish pass-only records. Historical certificate validity remains clear.
7. **Keep conversion paths ordinary.** Work, CV and Contact remain standard links
   in navigation; downloads do not require a form, animation completion or login.
   Employer websites identify organizations; they do not prove individual authorship.

## Editorial guardrails

- Startup work means adapting priorities and delivering with founders, not
  disparaging previous work or claiming a formal leadership title.
- Infrastructure designed for thousands of users is a design goal, not verified
  active users, concurrency or a load-test result.
- Cooperativa's ten-minute baseline was manual permission lookup, not SQL latency.
- Details are complete through progressive disclosure, not by making every screen
  an exhaustive biography. Avoid repeating the full stack in every paragraph.
- A public portfolio can improve clarity and evidence access; it cannot guarantee
  recruiter interest. Real conversion and field performance are not measured here.

## Verification

Use catalogue/evidence tests, bilingual rendered checks and browser regressions

actual screenshots: passing lint is not visual QA. No new analytics or external
