Docker Compose uses the existing Docker mark; it does not introduce a duplicate
brand in the hero. No independent pgvector brand asset was found in its upstream
repository; its labelled data icon remains. Generic concepts retain descriptive
category icons rather than invented logos.

Simple Icons is CC0, but its [disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md)
expressly separates icon licensing from brand rights. The historical AWS and Teams
assets are pinned to 11.15.0, not presented as the newest corporate identities.
AWS architecture and Microsoft trademark rules continue to apply. Karate's asset
comes from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), under its repository
license and individual brand conditions. Other assets originate in the named
projects' sites or repositories; no additional reuse licence is claimed here.

Verification: all SVGs were checked for executable elements, embedded images,
event handlers and external references; raster files have valid PNG signatures
and adequate intrinsic dimensions. A local 64px contact sheet was visually
reviewed for recognizable shapes, correct proportions and contrast on the white
logo tiles used by both themes. Corporate artwork was not redrawn with AI.
