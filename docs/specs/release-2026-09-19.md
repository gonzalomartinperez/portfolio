## Deliberate boundaries

- No artificial charts, decorative skeletons, paid component purchases or MUI.
- No changes to hosting, Cloudflare DNS, repository protection or external profiles.
- No claim of perfect equivalence with a dynamic third-party animation, guaranteed
  recruitment outcomes, physical-mobile frame rates or field Web Vitals.
- Preserve the approved 43-image Filomena gallery; do not silently publish other
  screenshots or private professional materials.
- LinkedIn copy is prepared separately; publishing LinkedIn remains manual.
- Unknown URLs share a bilingual recovery page. In the installed Next.js version,
  the tested Spanish catch-all returned an empty server error shell without
  JavaScript; the Suspense alternative returned HTTP 200. Both were rejected.
  The root 404 preserves the correct status and server-rendered navigation.
